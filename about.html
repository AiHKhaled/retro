About Khaled Ait Hammou

My name is Khaled Ait Hammou, an Algeria-based Senior Frontend Engineer with 4+ years of experience delivering production web applications for government platforms and B2B systems. I specialize in React, Next.js, and TypeScript, with a focus on simplicity, usability, and reliable delivery.

I currently lead frontend work across institutional platforms at EADN. My work also extends into API architecture, authentication, data flow, reusable component systems, and the technical decisions needed to take a product from an empty repository to production.

You can find a curated selection of that work on the projects page, including public government platforms, ticketing systems, mobile marketplaces, and e-commerce products.

Outside of work, my passions include photography, reading books, writing, watching obscure or poetic TV shows and movies, and tinkering with technology.

You can follow my work on LinkedIn and GitHub, or my photography on Instagram.
